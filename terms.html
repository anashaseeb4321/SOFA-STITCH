2. How to order

You browse our collections on the website and place your order by messaging us on WhatsApp, either by tapping an "Order" button or completing the order form, which sends your details to us on WhatsApp. We'll then confirm the details, size, fabric, price, and delivery with you directly.

Your order is an offer to buy from us. A contract is formed only once we confirm your order and agree to fulfil it. Until we confirm, we may decline an order — for example, if an item is out of stock, we can't deliver to your area, or there's an error in the price or description.

4. Pricing

Prices are shown in pounds sterling (£) and include free delivery within the United Kingdom, unless stated otherwise. We take care to price everything correctly, but if we spot a genuine error before your order is confirmed, we'll tell you and you can choose to continue at the correct price or cancel.

5. Payment — cash on delivery

All orders are paid by cash on delivery. There is no online payment. Payment is due in full, in cash, to the delivery driver at the point your order is delivered and you're happy to accept it. Because you pay on delivery, you can inspect your order at your door before paying — if it's not right, you can decline it and pay nothing.
