1. Check it at your door first

Every order is cash on delivery, so you can inspect your sofa before you pay a penny. If anything isn't right — the wrong item, visible damage, or you've simply changed your mind — you can decline it at the door and pay nothing. This is the quickest, easiest outcome for everyone, so please do check your order on arrival.

2. Your 14-day right to change your mind

For standard (non-custom) sofas, you have the right to cancel your order for any reason within 14 days of receiving it, under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013. You then have a further 14 days to return the item to us.

To use this right, the sofa must be unused and in the same condition you received it (see Condition of returned items). For change-of-mind returns, you are responsible for arranging and paying the cost of returning the item to us, unless we agree otherwise in writing.

3. Custom & made-to-measure orders

Please read this before ordering custom sizes. Sofas made to your own measurements, or in a fabric/colour chosen specifically for your order, are made to order for you. Under UK law, these bespoke items are not covered by the 14-day change-of-mind right and cannot be returned or refunded simply because you've changed your mind. Your rights if a custom item arrives faulty or not as described are unaffected — see below.

4. Faulty or damaged items

Separately from the above, everything we sell is covered by the Consumer Rights Act 2015. If your sofa is faulty, damaged, or not as described:

  • Damaged on delivery: if possible, decline it at the door. If damage is found after delivery, report it within 48 hours with photos.
  • Faulty within 30 days: you have the right to reject it for a full refund.
  • Faulty after 30 days: we'll repair or replace it. If that isn't possible, you may be entitled to a refund.

For faulty or damaged items, we cover the cost of collection or return — you won't pay to send it back.

5. How to start a return

Message us on WhatsApp with your name, order details, and — if the item is faulty or damaged — clear photos. We'll confirm whether your item qualifies and arrange the next steps, usually within 24 hours.

6. Condition of returned items

To be accepted for a change-of-mind return, the sofa must be:

  • Unused and clean, with no marks, odours, or signs of wear;
  • In its original packaging where possible;
  • Complete, including any accessories, stools, or tables ordered with it.

We may reduce your refund to reflect any loss in value caused by handling beyond what's needed to inspect the item, as permitted by law.

7. Return collection & who pays

  • Changed your mind: you arrange and pay for return delivery. We can arrange collection for you and deduct the cost from your refund — just ask.
  • Faulty, damaged, or wrong item: we arrange and pay for collection at no cost to you.

8. Refunds

Once we receive the returned sofa and confirm its condition, we'll issue your refund by bank transfer within 14 days. Because orders are paid in cash on delivery, please have your bank details ready so we can return your money quickly. If you declined the item at the door and paid nothing, there's nothing to refund.
